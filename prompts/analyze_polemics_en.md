# Polemical Text Analysis Task

**Objective:** Conduct a comprehensive analysis of the polemical dimensions in the provided text, with special attention to philosophical underpinnings and rhetorical effectiveness.

**Text:**  
{text}

## Analysis Framework:
1. **Controversy Mapping**:
   - Identify explicit and implicit controversial claims
   - Classify by type (ethical, metaphysical, political, etc.)

2. **Rhetorical Deconstruction**:
   - Analyze argument structure and logical flow
   - Identify persuasive techniques (ethos/pathos/logos balance)
   - Evaluate use of metaphor, irony, and other literary devices

3. **Philosophical Grounding**:
   - Trace connections to major philosophical traditions
   - Identify underlying assumptions and worldviews
